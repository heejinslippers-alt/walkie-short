## Facts (with sources)
- About 1.45-1.5 billion cars in the world (2024-25 estimates; JATO Dynamics via whichcar.com.au; Hedges & Company ~1.7 billion incl. commercial vehicles).
  https://www.whichcar.com.au/news/how-many-cars-are-there-in-the-world
- London drivers lost 91 hours each to congestion in 2025 (INRIX 2025 Global Traffic Scorecard, down 10 % from 2024).
  https://inrix.com/press-releases/2025-global-traffic-scorecard-uk/
- 2008, Nagoya (Sugiyama et al., New Journal of Physics): 22 cars on a 230 m ring asked to hold 30 km/h; a jam formed with no bottleneck
  and travelled backwards like a wave. https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2008/03/080304075707.htm
- 2017, Tucson (Stern, Work et al., Transportation Research Part C 2018): one autonomous car in a ring with 20+ human drivers dampened the
  stop-and-go waves. https://cee.illinois.edu/news/experiments-show-few-self-driving-cars-can-dramatically-improve-traffic-flow

## Script
What if every car on Earth drove at exactly the same speed? Would traffic jams disappear?
There are one and a half billion cars on the planet, and last year, London drivers lost ninety-one hours each, stuck in traffic.
So one morning, every car locks to the same speed.
Motorways flow like a river. Nobody tailgates. Nobody overtakes.
Then a traffic light turns red.
One car stops, so every car on Earth has to stop.
In Texas, a truck slams on the brakes. In Paris, a whole roundabout freezes.
Luckily, nobody ever crashes into the car in front.
Unluckily, every red light on the planet now stops the entire planet.
But jams don't even need a red light. In two thousand and eight, scientists in Japan had twenty-two cars drive in a circle at one steady speed.
Within minutes, a jam appeared out of nowhere.
One driver brakes a little. The one behind brakes a bit more. And the squeeze rolls backwards, like an accordion.
So the cure isn't equal speed. It's equal gaps.
In two thousand and seventeen, one self-driving car kept a steady gap in a ring of twenty, and the waves faded away.
So next time you're stuck in traffic, be that car.
Leave a gap.
