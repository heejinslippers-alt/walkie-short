## Script (193 words)
What if every billionaire on Earth exploded, and anyone who ever became one exploded too?
Right now, there are three thousand four hundred and twenty-eight billionaires, sitting on about twenty trillion dollars.
At noon tomorrow: pop. Pop. Pop.
Pool parties go quiet. Limousines wait outside, forever.
But the money doesn't vanish. It goes to their kids.
And the moment a kid inherits a billion: pop.
Then the grandkids. Pop, pop.
Lawyers start chopping fortunes into pieces as fast as they can.
Luckily, America's biggest lottery winner took the cash option: nine hundred and ninety-seven point six million dollars.
Unluckily, he's one good stock tip away from going pop.
It's hot potato with money. You only blow up if you're holding too much, so you pass it on.
Split three billion between four kids, and nobody pops.
So the rich would race to give it away. More than two hundred and fifty of them have already promised to.
But the rule never said which currency.
In two thousand and eight, Zimbabwe's money got so worthless that a loaf of bread cost billions of dollars.
Zimbabwe dollars.
The whole bakery queue would have gone pop.
